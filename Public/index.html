GRC case study: Lone Star Logistics

A fictitious mid-sized freight and logistics company (about 450 employees) used as the setting for three connected governance, risk and compliance projects, aligned to NIST CSF v2.0.

  • Policy suite: Access Control, Incident Response and Vendor Risk Management policies
  • Risk register and gap assessment: four risks mapped to policy gaps and a documented incident
  • System hardening auditor: a Python script based on CIS benchmarks, run against Ubuntu 22.04 until all six automated checks passed
  • Network lab: a Cisco Packet Tracer design with a hub and two spoke sites, DHCP relay, and OSPF with static failover
  • NIST CSF 2.0
  • Risk assessment
  • CIS benchmarks
  • Python
  • Cisco Packet Tracer
